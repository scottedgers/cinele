    How to Play CINELE
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
          CINELE is a daily cinema deduction game. Identify the mystery film in 10 guesses or fewer by cross-referencing directors, composers, genres, and cast.
        
    

        
    
          
    
            
    01
    
            
    
              Lock In Any Film
              
    Search and select any movie from the curated catalog of 600+ iconic films.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    02
    
            
    
              Decode the 6 Telemetry Tiles
              
    Each guess reveals how your film compares to the mystery target across 6 production categories.
    
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    Signal Colors & Meaning
    
        
    
          
    
            🟩 Exact
            
    
              Exact Match
              
    Same Release Year, same Director(s), same Composer(s), exact same Genres, same Source Material, or same Primary Lead Actor.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            🟨 Partial
            
    
              Close / Partial Match
              
      
                
    • Release Year: Within ±5 years of the mystery movie.
      • 
                
    • Director: Shares a co-director credit.
      • 
                
    • Composer: Shares a co-composer or music collaborator.
      • 
                
    • Genre: Shares 1 of the 2 genres with the mystery movie.
      • 
                
    • Source: Both are written fiction adaptations (Novel ↔ Short Story ↔ Comic) or stage/folklore adaptations.
      • 
                
    • Lead Cast: An actor in your guess's Top-2 cast appears in the mystery film's Top-2 cast.
      • 
              
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            🟥 Miss
            
    
              No Match
              
    More than 5 years apart, or no overlap in that category.
    
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    Timeline Arrows (↑ / ↓)
    
        
    
          In the Release Year column, ↑ means the mystery movie was released later (newer) than your guess, and ↓ means it was released earlier (older).
        
    

        
    ⭐ 100-Point Score, Clues & Film Archive
    
        
    
          
    
            100 PTS
            
    
              10-Guess Score Scale
              
    Every round starts at a 100/100 Score across 10 guesses. Each incorrect guess deducts -8 pts. Even if you run out of guesses, you earn salvage points (+3 per 🟩 Exact category, +1 per 🟨 Partial category unlocked).
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            💡 HINTS
            
    
              Clue Assists (Year, Director & Lead Cast)
              
    Need a nudge? Reveal the mystery film's Release Year (-6 pts), Director (-8 pts), or Lead Cast (-10 pts) at any time, or unlock the Film Archive (-12 pts). Solve with zero hints or archive help to earn the 🧠 Pure Memory badge!
