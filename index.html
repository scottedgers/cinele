    🎟️ How to Play CINEDLE
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Inspired by Theaterle, CINEDLE challenges you to guess the mystery film in 8 tries or fewer!
        
    

        
    
          
    
            
    1
    
            
    
              Make a Guess
              
    Search and select any movie from the catalog of 600+ iconic films.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    2
    
            
    
              Read the 6 Clue Tiles
              
    After each guess, 6 category tiles flip over and change color to show how close your guess is to the mystery film.
    
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    Clue Colors & Meaning
    
        
    
          
    
            🟩 Green
            
    
              Exact Match!
              
    Same Release Year, same Director(s), same Composer(s), exact same Genres, same Source Material, or same Primary Lead Actor.
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            🟨 Gold
            
    
              Close / Partial Match!
              
      
                
    • Release Year: Within ±5 years of the mystery movie.
      • 
                
    • Director: Shares a co-director credit.
      • 
                
    • Sound Composer: Shares a co-composer or music collaborator.
      • 
                
    • Genre: Shares 1 of the 2 genres with the mystery movie.
      • 
                
    • Source: Both are written fiction adaptations (Novel ↔ Short Story ↔ Comic) or stage/folklore adaptations.
      • 
                
    • Lead Actor: An actor in your guess's Top-2 cast appears in the mystery film's Top-2 cast!
      • 
              
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            🟥 Burgundy
            
    
              No Match
              
    More than 5 years apart, or no overlap in that category.
    
            
    
          
    
        
    

        
    Arrows (↑ / ↓)
    
        
    
          In the Release Year column, ↑ means the mystery movie was released later (newer) than your guess, and ↓ means it was released earlier (older).
        
    

        
    ⭐ 100-Point Critic Score & Film Archive
    
        
    
          
    
            100 pts
            
    
              Critic Score Scale
              
    Every round starts at a 100/100 Critic Score. Each incorrect guess deducts -10 pts. Even if you run out of guesses, you earn salvage points (+3 per 🟩 Green category, +1 per 🟨 Gold category unlocked).
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            📖 -15 pts
            
    
              Film Archive Assist vs. 🧠 Pure Memory
              
    Play from memory to earn the 🧠 Pure Memory badge! Opening the Film Archive during an active round applies a one-time -15 pt deduction and marks the guess row on your shareable emoji card with 📖.
